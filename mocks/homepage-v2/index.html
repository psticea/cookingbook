Homepage v2 — three proposals

Three independent redesigns of the Paul's Cookbook homepage. All three keep the two-column recipe grid, the current type scale, the right-hand side menu, and every piece of information the live homepage exposes. Open each one and resize to 390 × 844 to judge it the way it will be used.

Data comes from the real catalogue via shared/catalogue.js. Regenerate it with node mocks/homepage-v2/shared/prepare.mjs. See BRIEF.md for the design contract.
